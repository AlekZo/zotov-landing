Problems we can tackle

These are possible development directions, not off-the-shelf products or claims about completed projects. We define the scope around your workflow.

Enquiry handling

Qualify leads in Telegram, answer common questions and offer Google Calendar bookings without a person handling every message.

Documents from your data

Draft proposals, contracts and completion certificates as PDFs from a form or CRM instead of filling the same templates repeatedly.

Customer portals

Frequently asked questions

A few practical details about working together.

What do you build?
Websites and catalogues, web applications, customer portals, internal systems and integrations that support real business workflows.
Can you connect a solution to a CRM, Telegram and AI?
Yes. We define integrations and useful AI tasks after understanding the workflow and checking technical constraints.
Where can the website or application run?
It can run on the client's own servers, a dedicated VPS or cloud infrastructure. We choose an option based on data, access and maintenance requirements.
What happens after launch?
I can help operate, fix and improve the solution. We agree the scope and terms of ongoing support separately.
How much will my project cost?
The page shows starting budget guidance by project scale. I prepare a specific estimate after discussing workflows, features and integrations.

Tell me what needs to change.

A couple of sentences are enough: what is not working now, what you need to build, and which tools it should connect to. I will reply with a sensible next step.
